    Overwatch Esports

    Welcome to the new world of competitive Overwatch! Being part of overwatch esports has never been easier with new premiere and amateur circuits, including the Overwatch Champions Series (OWCS), Calling All Heroes (CAH), Overwatch Collegiate, Overwatch Scholastic, FACEIT League, through support by our partnered FACEIT, Start.GG and WaraGG platforms.

    OWCS

    Overwatch Champions Series

    Overwatch Champions Series (OWCS) is the premier competitive structure that lets players and fans experience Overwatch Esports like never before. Showcasing top-tier competition across four major regions: North America (NA), EMEA (Europe Middle East & North Africa), Asia (Korea, Japan, Pacific), and China. The competition will include incredible storylines and enviable viewership rewards making Overwatch Champions series one of the world’s most exciting Esports series.
    Overwatch Champions Series is run in partnership with FACEIT Group, WDG and NetEase using their respective platforms.

    FAQ

    ANY QUESTIONS?

    What type of tournament is OWCS?
    OWCS is an open ecosystem Overwatch esports program where the best teams across each region will compete in Regional Competition to earn the right to represent their region in international Live Events, crowning our Season champion at the end of the year.
    When will the tournament take place?
    The OWCS 2025 Season will kick off in January with the OWCS 2025 Open Qualifier. Head here to read more on the 2025 Season format and structure.
    Where do I tune in to OWCS?
    OWCS will broadcast the 2025 Season on YouTube, Twitch, and X. Follow OWE channels to stay up to date if this changes.
    When can I register for OWCS in NA & EMEA?
    Players may register for the OWCS 2025 - NA or EMEA Open Qualifier now, or FACEIT Leagues by creating an account on FACEIT. Head here to learn more on OWCS 2025 Open Qualifier or FACEIT Leagues.
    HOW DO I LEARN MORE ABOUT THE OWCS 2025 Team Partner Program?
    Head here to our OWCS 2025 Announcement blog to learn more.
    When can I register for OWCS in ASIA?
    Head to OWCS Asia channels here to learn more on how to register.
